How one recall() executes

Candidate generation optimises recall; everything after fusion optimises precision per token. The two filters in orange are correctness gates, not quality heuristics.

Objective function. The target is not recall@k — it is answer accuracy per token of assembled context. Long context does not remove the need for memory: lost-in-the-middle costs >30% mid-context, and all 18 frontier models tested in the context-rot study degrade monotonically with input length.
